## What happened
The Support lead (phantom8817) passed on a customer report in #support: order ORD-100953 was charged twice. The Fixer found the cause: charge_order retried after a provider timeout with no idempotency key; the first charge had already gone through, so the retry charged again.

## Who was affected
Per the Investigator: 313 customers were double charged, $21,607.00 USD in total. 1 complained; 312 had not said anything yet.

## The fix
Every attempt for an order now sends the same key (`charge:<order_id>`), and the provider returns the original charge on a repeat. Committed on `fix/double-charge` with regression test `test_timeout_then_retry_charges_once` (pytest: `13 passed, 1 warning in 0.42s`). Approved by phantom8817.
